AmpTrack for Android

Privacy Policy

AmpTrack is developed and operated by Qasim Zaib. This policy explains how information is handled in the first-release version with cloud sync disabled.

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · Policy version: 2026-10-06-v1

Your charging history stays on your device. Google sign-in, account and device management, and crash reporting still use online services. Signing in does not provide a cloud backup.

1. Information the app uses

Account information
When you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication handles sign-in. AmpTrack stores an account identifier and may store your email address, display name, profile-photo URL, and account creation or sign-in times. These support your account, device management, and privacy requests. AmpTrack does not receive your Google password. Firebase may also process connection information, such as IP addresses and user-agent information, for authentication and abuse prevention.
Device and installation information
AmpTrack registers your device using app-generated device and installation identifiers, device name, manufacturer/model, Android version and build details, and registration or activity times. This supports device identification, registration, and account safety. The app does not request your IMEI, device serial number, Android ID, MAC address, or phone number as a device identifier.
Charging information stored on your device
AmpTrack records battery level, current, voltage, temperature, charging/plug state, screen on/off state, and timestamps. It also stores session history, graphs, and the charger/cable details and notes you enter. These support battery-side charging estimates and comparisons. User-enabled monitoring can continue while the app is in the background, with an ongoing notification. Screen state means whether the screen is on or off; it is not a recording of screen contents.
Crash diagnostics
The production app uses Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and improve reliability. Reports can include crash traces, technical logs, app/build information, device and operating-system details, and installation identifiers.
Support and deletion requests
If you email us or request deletion, we process the information needed to identify your account, verify ownership, respond, and complete the request. This includes your email address, message, relevant account identifiers, request status, and limited processing records.

2. Local history, sync, and exports

Cloud sync is unavailable in this release. The app does not perform ordinary uploads or downloads of charging sessions, raw readings, graphs, charger/cable details, or notes for cloud backup. Account, device, and privacy-request traffic remains active.

History is stored in the app's local database. Android app-data backup is disabled for AmpTrack. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app can permanently remove this history. Signing in again does not restore it.

When you choose to export, AmpTrack creates a CSV file in its cache and lets you share it through an app you select. The receiving app or person then handles that copy. CSV export is not a restorable AmpTrack backup.

3. Services and sharing

We use Google/Firebase for authentication, account and device records, privacy requests, and crash reporting. Information needed for these services is processed by those providers. We do not sell personal information. This release does not contain advertising or Google Analytics integration.

Support email is handled through Microsoft Outlook/Hotmail. This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages; the host may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to provide and protect the website. These pages do not add advertising or analytics scripts.

Providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their privacy information is available here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft Privacy Statement
  • GitHub Privacy Statement

4. Retention and deletion

Local history and exported files
Local history remains until you delete it or the app's storage is cleared. Automatic age-based pruning of raw charging readings is suspended in this version. Cached CSV files can be removed through privacy cleanup or app-storage clearing; creating another export also attempts cleanup of older cached exports. Copies you saved or shared outside AmpTrack are not removed by the app.
AmpTrack account and cloud records
We retain your account and associated cloud records while the account is active. After verifying an account-deletion request, we complete deletion of the AmpTrack account and its associated records in our active application systems within seven days. This includes the account profile, registered device records, and any existing account-linked cloud history. Provider-managed backups and diagnostic records follow the separate lifecycles below.
Firebase authentication records and backups
Firebase documents that logged authentication IP addresses are retained for a few weeks. After account deletion is initiated with Firebase, other authentication information is removed from its live and backup systems within 180 days.
Crash diagnostics
Firebase documents a 90-day retention period for crash traces and associated identifiers before starting removal from live and backup systems. Deleting your AmpTrack account does not immediately erase all previously submitted crash diagnostics. See the linked Firebase privacy information for its current retention practices.
Request correspondence and processing records
Support emails and limited records of deletion requests are kept only while needed to handle the request, verify completion, or resolve related follow-up. We review these records and delete them when they are no longer needed.

5. Your choices

You can stop monitoring using the app or its notification, use the app's local-data deletion controls, choose whether to export data, and request deletion of your AmpTrack account. Clearing local data or uninstalling AmpTrack does not by itself delete the account held by our online services.

To request account deletion, use Settings → Account → Request account deletion, or follow the instructions on our account-deletion page. Email requests do not require installing or signing into the app. We verify ownership to protect accounts from unauthorized deletion.

Account deletion affects your AmpTrack account, not your Google account. Local cleanup on other installations may wait until those installations reconnect and successfully check account status; an offline device cannot be wiped immediately.

For access, correction, or other privacy requests, contact zaib_qasim@hotmail.com. Any additional rights depend on the laws that apply to you.

6. Security

The app uses encrypted connections for Firebase communications and account-based access controls for online records. Local data is stored within Android's app storage. Access to support correspondence and deletion-processing records is restricted to handling those requests.

7. Changes and contact

We will update this policy when data practices change, including before introducing cloud sync or advertising. The current version and update date appear at the top of this page.
