Delete your AmpTrack account

You can request deletion from the app or by email. You do not need to reinstall AmpTrack to make an email request.

Developer: Qasim Zaib · Last updated: 6 October 2026

Within seven days after ownership verification. We manually process deletion of your AmpTrack account and associated records in our active application systems. Provider backups and diagnostics have separate retention periods, explained below.

Request deletion by email

  1. Email zaib_qasim@hotmail.com with the subject AmpTrack account deletion request.
  2. Include the Google email address you used to sign into AmpTrack and say that you want your AmpTrack account and associated data deleted.
  3. We will confirm ownership through the email address associated with the account. If you cannot access that address, contact us for help with verification.
  4. After verification, we will complete the active-account deletion within seven days and send confirmation for your email request.
Open deletion request email

The button opens your email app; it does not submit a request until you send the email. If it does not open, copy the address and instructions above into your email service. Never send your Google password, sign-in token, or recovery codes.

Or request deletion in the app

Open Settings → Account → Request account deletion and follow the confirmation and Google reauthentication steps. You need an internet connection for the request to be accepted. Accepted requests are manually processed; acceptance is not the same as completed deletion. You can email us for a status update.

What is deleted

  • Your AmpTrack account and its Firebase Authentication identity.
  • Your account profile, including stored name, email, and profile-photo URL.
  • Registered device records and related account data.
  • Any existing charging-history or other content stored under your AmpTrack cloud account, even though cloud sync is disabled in this version.

This does not delete your Google account. Deletion is permanent for the affected AmpTrack records. Signing in again after deletion can create a new, empty AmpTrack account.

Local history and exported copies

An accepted in-app deletion request clears the requesting installation's account-local records and cached exports. Other installations may clear account-local data when they next reconnect and successfully check account status. Email deletion cannot immediately wipe a device that is offline.

You can also clear AmpTrack's storage using Android settings or uninstall the app to remove its local history. Neither action alone deletes your online AmpTrack account. Copies of CSV files already saved or shared outside the app must be deleted separately.

Records with separate retention

  • Authentication and backups: Firebase's account-deletion cleanup can take up to 180 days; authentication IP logs remain for a few weeks.
  • Crash diagnostics: Firebase starts removing crash reports and associated identifiers after 90 days. Account deletion does not immediately purge them.
  • Request records: Limited support correspondence and deletion-processing records are kept only while needed to handle the request, verify completion, or resolve related follow-up, and are reviewed and deleted when no longer needed.

These separate records do not keep your AmpTrack account active. For more detail, see our privacy policy's retention section and Firebase's privacy information.
